# Homepage assets

Typography: Inter by the Inter Project Authors, self-hosted from the
`@fontsource-variable/inter` package. Its SIL Open Font License is included in
that package. One font family is used throughout the site for a consistent,
readable interface.

Editorial imagery (illustrative spaces, not property listings):
- https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1600607687939-ce8a6c25118c
- https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1600210492486-724fe5c67fb0

Property cards use the actual agency-uploaded or linked photos. Missing or failed
photos receive a neutral placeholder rather than an illustrative property photo.
